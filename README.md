OSMI Mental Health Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the OSMI (Open Sourcing Mental Illness) Mental Health in Tech Survey dataset to understand mental health trends, workplace support, and treatment-seeking behavior among employees in the tech industry.

The project focuses on identifying factors affecting employee mental health and generating insights that can help organizations build healthier and more supportive workplaces.

---

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze mental health trends in the tech industry
- Understand factors influencing treatment-seeking behavior
- Identify workplace conditions affecting mental health
- Explore demographic and organizational patterns
- Generate actionable business insights

🔍 Key Insights

1️⃣ Mental Health Treatment Analysis

- A significant proportion of employees reported seeking mental health treatment.
- Mental health awareness is increasing in the tech industry, but stigma still exists.

---

2️⃣ Gender-wise Analysis

- Female and non-binary employees showed higher treatment-seeking behavior compared to male employees.
- Indicates possible underreporting or stigma among male employees.

---

3️⃣ Family History Impact

- Employees with a family history of mental illness were more likely to seek treatment.
- Family background showed a strong influence on mental health awareness.

---

4️⃣ Workplace Benefits Analysis

- Employees with access to mental health benefits were more likely to seek support and treatment.
- Organizations providing mental health resources showed healthier workplace behavior.

---

5️⃣ Work Interference Analysis

- Employees experiencing frequent work interference due to mental health conditions showed higher treatment rates.
- Demonstrates the impact of mental health on workplace productivity.

---

6️⃣ Age Group Trends

- Younger professionals showed greater mental health awareness.
- Certain age groups displayed higher stress and treatment patterns.
